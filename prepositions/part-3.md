## Comparative Table 3 — Causation, Means, Manner, Relation & Association Functions

**Note:** English, French, Italian, and Arabic mainly use prepositions. Japanese expresses these relations through particles, postpositions, and case constructions.

---

# Causation Examples

| Function   | English                                   | French                                         | Italian                                            | Arabic                         | Japanese            |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------- |
